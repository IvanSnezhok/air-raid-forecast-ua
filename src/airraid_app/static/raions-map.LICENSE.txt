raions-map.json: simplified raion (district) boundaries of Ukraine.

Source: Ukraine - Subnational Administrative Boundaries (COD-AB), version v05,
valid on 2025-09-01, published by OCHA on the Humanitarian Data Exchange:
https://data.humdata.org/dataset/cod-ab-ukr
Original data: State Scientific Production Enterprise "Kartographia" (Ukraine).
License: Creative Commons Attribution for Intergovernmental Organisations
(CC BY 3.0 IGO), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/igo/

Changes: projected to screen coordinates, simplified (shared borders simplified
once, 0.4 px tolerance at 1000 px width), the Chornobyl exclusion zone merged
into Vyshhorodskyi raion, islets smaller than ~0.05 px^2 dropped. Built by
scripts/build_map.py.

alerts.in.ua location UIDs in src/airraid/raions.py are the published location
ids of alerts.in.ua (also listed in the MIT-licensed alerts_in_ua Python client,
https://github.com/alerts-ua/alerts-in-ua-py).
